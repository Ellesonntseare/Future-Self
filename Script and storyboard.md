# A Conversation with My Future Self

60-second working draft. Fictional character: Alex, shown as a younger and older adult. Realistic illustrated artwork, soft cinematic light, calm natural movement.

## 1. The unfinished page · 0–8 seconds

Late evening. A young creator sits with an unfinished notebook. A quiet breath before speaking.

**Young Alex:** I thought I'd be further by now.

## 2. A familiar stranger · 8–16 seconds

An older version appears nearby. Warm light gently enters the room.

**Older Alex:** You always did.

**Young Alex:** Do I know you?

**Older Alex:** Better than anyone.

## 3. Recognition · 16–24 seconds

A close look at the same eyes, familiar clothes and greying hair. Surprise softens into curiosity.

**Young Alex:** You're... me?

**Older Alex:** With a few more grey hairs.

**Young Alex:** Then tell me. Does it work out?

## 4. An honest answer · 24–33 seconds

The older self gives a small, honest smile. No grand promise.

**Older Alex:** Not the way you planned.

**Young Alex:** So why keep going?

**Older Alex:** Do you remember why you started?

## 5. Remembering · 33–43 seconds

The younger self looks toward the unfinished page. Their expression begins to lift.

**Young Alex:** I wanted to make something that mattered.

**Older Alex:** That part of you is still here.

## 6. One small thing · 43–55 seconds

A gentle hand gesture toward the notebook. The younger self turns back to their work.

**Older Alex:** Start with one small thing. It doesn't have to be perfect. It just has to be yours.

## 7. We try again · 55–60 seconds

A shared smile. The younger self begins a new line. Hold the hopeful final frame.

**Young Alex:** And if I fail?

**Older Alex:** Then we try again.
